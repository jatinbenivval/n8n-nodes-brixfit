# v1.4.0 — 2026-10-04

Easier setup, smarter lead fields, and new Form and Onboarding operations. Existing workflows keep working with no changes.

> Works with the latest Brixfit. If you self-host or use an older version, update Brixfit first.

## Improved

- **Simpler setup.** Paste your API key, click Save, and n8n confirms the connection straight away. A short guide is shown right inside the credential.
- **Lead fields match your lead form.** Create and Update now show your form's own labels, dropdown choices and required markers (`*`). Required rules follow your form exactly, so you are only asked for what a visitor would be asked for.
- **Clearer messages.** If something needs attention, the node names the exact fields and tells you where to adjust them in Brixfit.
- **Lead → Update** now applies your custom form fields.
- Leads can be sent with `full_name` as well as `name`, and with a question's standard name (for example `weight`) as well as your own question key.

## New

- **Form** resource — **Get Many** and **Get** for your lead and onboarding forms, including the public form link, questions, choices and conditions.
- **Client → Get Onboarding** — see a client's onboarding progress, outstanding profile details and submitted answers.
- **New events** — `lead.call_booked` and `client.deleted` are available in the Trigger and in Webhook → Create.
- "Get All" is now labelled **Get Many**, following n8n conventions. Your saved workflows are unaffected.
- Refreshed Brixfit icon.

## Under the hood

- Code reorganised into smaller modules to make the node easier to maintain and extend.
